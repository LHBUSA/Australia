RESPONSE DISCIPLINE
Three signals matter more than a 200.
A successful request means the service answered. It does not mean every jurisdiction-native source layer exists for that property.
01 · MATCH
What actually resolved?
Read match_level and the returned jurisdiction. Address identity, coordinate containment and source-native identifiers are different evidence.
02 · COVERAGE
How deep can you safely go?
Use coverage_status and layer-specific availability. A valid base property can survive while optional cadastral or geometry depth is unavailable.
03 · PROVENANCE
Where did the fact come from?
Preserve source name, dataset and revision context. PropData normalises access without erasing Australian source meaning.
